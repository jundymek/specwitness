### Testing requirements

- **TDD, red first, and every guard seen red.** Epic 4 retro §2 observation 7 and §3 lesson 7: **a guard is only a guard once you have seen it fail.** Two vacuous tests in one Epic 4 story both passed against unfixed code and were caught only by planting the failure. **State in your Dev Agent Record what you planted and what went red.**
- **⚠️ H-8 HAS TEETH IN THIS STORY MORE THAN ANY OTHER.** The auto-review runs `pnpm test` **concurrently in your worktree**, and **a Playwright suite is the most expensive thing anyone has yet put in that path.** Therefore:
  - **The suite must not download a browser.** Provisioning is tested by driving the resolution and refusal logic with an injected runner and fixture directories — not by pulling hundreds of megabytes twice, concurrently, on the reviewer's machine. If any test genuinely needs a real browser, it must be **opt-in** (an env flag, off by default) and must say so loudly.
  - **Ephemeral ports and temp directories only.** A hardcoded port or a fixed cache path fails against a concurrent copy of your own suite.
  - **When no browser is available, a browser-requiring test may skip — with a loud, counted reason.** ⚠️ **A skipped TEST and a skipped CRITERION are different things and conflating them is observation 2 arriving a third time.** A test skip means "this machine could not exercise this"; a criterion skip means "nothing adjudicated this and we called it green." **Your production code has no skip path at all.** Say this in the test file's header so nobody later "helpfully" makes the executor skip to match the suite.
