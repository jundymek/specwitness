## Dev Notes

### Goal & Context

**Your fixtures defend the boundary that makes SpecWitness trustworthy rather than merely noisy.**

A verification gate that reports FAIL when a port was busy gets switched off within a week. The product's four-way separation — PASS / FAIL / NEEDS_HUMAN / infrastructure error — is what earns it the right to be mandatory, and it is the very first thing `CLAUDE.md` lists under non-negotiable product rules: *"Infra failures are never reported as product FAIL."*

Five epics have asserted that in unit tests. **No test has ever driven the real binary through a genuinely unstartable service and checked what came out.** That is your fixture 8, and its mirror is fixture 7 — a broken build that must stay a product FAIL and must not be excused as infrastructure. Getting these two wrong in opposite directions is how a gate becomes either ignorable or unfair, and neither failure announces itself.

### Expectation revisions — what I originally wrote, and why I was wrong

**Revision 1 — `09-meta-only-change`: I wrote `stderrAbsent: ["integrity"]`, and it
was wrong.**

I reasoned that a clean run should never mention integrity at all. Running it said
otherwise, and the product was right:

```
✓ ok          integrity         0 ms  epic-1 v2 verified against its fingerprint (1 criteria)
```

`integrity` is the **name of the stage**, and that line is printed on every healthy
run. My expectation would have demanded the product stay silent about a check that
had just succeeded.

The fix made the fixture **stronger, not weaker**: the line moved from `stderrAbsent`
to `stderrContains`. Absence of a complaint is also what a check that never *ran*
would produce; the positive assertion distinguishes "integrity verified this contract
and was satisfied" from "integrity was skipped" — a distinction a corpus about
integrity must not blur. The two sentences only a real tamper can produce stayed in
`stderrAbsent`.

**Revision 2 — the ADR-008 branch: my first version silently weakened an AD-2
security guard. This is the one to read.**

I implemented ADR-008 as written: in `parseRunResult`, if every validation issue is an
unrecognised key, report a version skew. That broke a merged test —
`result-explanation.test.ts`, *"rejects a document whose explanation carries an unknown
key"* — whose own comment explains why it exists:

> a payload that smuggled a `status` alongside a hypothesis is the exact shape AD-2
> exists to make impossible, and it must be refused on READ as well as never written.

Under my change that document reported **"this stored run result was written by a newer
SpecWitness … Upgrade specwitness"**. A provider smuggling a verdict-shaped field into
a payload AD-2 declares non-authoritative would have been answered with an upgrade
prompt, sending the operator away from the only document that recorded the attempt.

**Why I was wrong:** ADR-008's premise — an unrecognised key means a newer writer — is
true of the **envelope**, where additive evolution actually happens (5.5 added
`explanations`, 5.6 `adaptation`, 5.4 `flakiness`), and false of a sub-object whose
**content came from a provider**. ADR-008 already draws that line: its Context excludes
`adaptation` as *"a provider input boundary, which this ADR deliberately does not
touch"*. It simply did not notice that `jsonReport`, which it **does** assign to this
story, *contains* provider-authored sub-objects.

**The fix** honours the carve-out at the reader — where ADR-008 §2 explicitly puts this
judgement (*"a property of the reader, not of the schema"*) — via
`PROVIDER_AUTHORED_PAYLOADS` in `src/schemas/result.ts`. The branch became **narrower
than the ADR's literal text, never wider**; everything excluded keeps merged
fail-closed behaviour. **I modified no merged test**, which is the evidence that the
narrowing is correct rather than convenient, and added two tests pinning both sides so
it cannot be widened back silently.

**Flagged as an ADR-008 gap for the retrospective.** I did not amend the ADR: a fixture
story editing an ADR's Decision section is the silent redesign `CLAUDE.md` forbids.

**Revision 3 — my `validRunResult()` test helper was wrong**, missing `baseSha`,
`headSha`, `stages` and `evidence` and carrying a `timeline` key that does not exist.
Caught by the deliberate control case (*"parses the valid document, so every case below
alters exactly one thing"*). Without that control, every skew assertion would have
passed for the wrong reason — the document failing validation for a defect the test
never intended to plant. The control earned its place immediately.
